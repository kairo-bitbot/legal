Privacy Policy for Pawdo: Pet Care for Dog & Cat

Effective October 7, 2026. Pawdo: Pet Care for Dog & Cat is published by Usama Moin ("we").

What the app stores

Pawdo stores pets, reminders and activity logs on your device in a local database. Household data (pet info, reminders and logs shared with household members) is synced with iCloud CloudKit, stored in your own iCloud account in a private database that is shared with household members through iCloud. We do not have access to this data. Pet photos, if added, are resized to 512 pixels and stored on your device and in your iCloud share.

What leaves your device

  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, never your name or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Usage events: TelemetryDeck receives anonymous events about which parts of the app are used, such as opening the app, finishing onboarding, logging a task and viewing the subscription screen. Each event carries a random identifier created on your device and hashed before it is sent. No names, pet details, notes or other personal data are included, and the events are not used for advertising or tracking. See telemetrydeck.com/privacy.
  • Crash reports: Sentry receives crash reports and session health (whether a session ended in a crash) to help us fix problems. These reports may include your device type and OS version, never your app data or personal details. We do not record your screen or your sessions. See sentry.io/privacy.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. Household data is controlled entirely by you and does not leave your iCloud account.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. To remove household data from iCloud, delete the household in the app's Settings tab or sign out of iCloud in your phone Settings. You can also remove the data through iCloud settings on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

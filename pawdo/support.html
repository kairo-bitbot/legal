Pawdo: Pet Care for Dog & Cat Support

Never miss a walk, meal or med again: reminders that actually ring, for every pet you love.

Restore your purchase

Open the app and go to Settings. Tap Restore Purchases and sign in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Cancel your subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Find Pawdo: Pet Care for Dog & Cat and tap Cancel Subscription.

Household help

iCloud is required for household sharing. Make sure iCloud is turned on in your phone Settings. To leave a household, open the app, go to Settings and tap Leave Household. To remove members or delete the household, use the Household tab in the app.

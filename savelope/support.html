Savelope Support

Tear open a paper envelope each day, watch your Savelope fill, and save together. No bank, no ads.

Restore your purchase

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase or subscribe.

Cancel your subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Find Savelope and tap Cancel Subscription.

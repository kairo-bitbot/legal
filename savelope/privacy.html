Privacy Policy for Savelope

Effective October 11, 2026. Savelope is published by Usama Moin ("we").

What the app stores

Savelope stores your challenges, entries and settings on your device in a local database. This data also syncs with iCloud CloudKit in your own private iCloud account. We do not have access to this data. Shared challenges are synced through an iCloud zone shared with your partner, which you control.

What leaves your device

  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, never your name or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Usage events: TelemetryDeck receives anonymous events about which parts of the app are used, such as opening the app, finishing onboarding, and drawing an envelope. Each event carries a random identifier created on your device. No amounts, challenge details, notes or other personal data are included, and the events are not used for advertising or tracking. See telemetrydeck.com/privacy.
  • Crash reports: Sentry receives crash reports and session health (whether a session ended in a crash) to help us fix problems. These reports may include your device type and OS version, never your app data or personal details. We do not record your screen or your sessions. See sentry.io/privacy.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. Savelope is a tracker. Your money stays wherever you keep it. We never ask for bank account details, move money or give financial advice.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. To remove data from iCloud, delete challenges in the app's Board tab or sign out of iCloud in your phone Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Privacy Policy for Savelope

Effective October 11, 2026. Savelope is published by Usama Moin ("we").

What the app stores

Savelope stores your challenges, entries and settings on your device in a local database. This data also syncs with iCloud CloudKit in your own private iCloud account. We do not have access to this data. Shared challenges are synced through an iCloud zone shared with your partner, which you control. People you share a challenge with can see its entries, who made them, and the display name you chose in the app. A small summary of your challenges is also kept in storage shared only with Savelope's home screen widget on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and never leave it.

What leaves your device

  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, never your name or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Usage events: TelemetryDeck receives anonymous events about which parts of the app are used, such as opening the app, finishing onboarding, putting an envelope aside (with the kind of challenge, such as 100 envelopes or 52 weeks), viewing the paywall, and starting, buying or restoring a plan. Each event carries a random identifier created on your device. No amounts, challenge names, notes, partner names or other personal data are included, and the events are not used for advertising or tracking. See telemetrydeck.com/privacy.
  • Crash reports: Sentry receives crash reports and session health (whether a session ended in a crash) to help us fix problems. These reports may include your device type and OS version, never your app data or personal details. We do not record your screen or your sessions, and we do not attach screenshots. See sentry.io/privacy.

Apple, RevenueCat, TelemetryDeck and Sentry each handle this data under their own privacy terms, and we only use providers that protect it to the standard described here. We keep usage events and crash reports only as long as needed to fix problems and understand how the app is used. To ask us to delete anything tied to you, or to withdraw consent to usage and crash data, email hello@usamamoin.com.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. Savelope is a tracker. Your money stays wherever you keep it. We never ask for bank account details, move money or give financial advice.

Deleting your data

Savelope has no accounts, so there is nothing to close. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. Archive a challenge in the Board tab and it is removed for good after 30 days. To delete what Savelope keeps in your iCloud, open iOS Settings, tap your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Savelope, then Delete Data. If you shared a challenge, your partner keeps their own copy, and you can stop sharing from the Together tab.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

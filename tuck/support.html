Support · Tuck

Tuck Support

Tuck saves anything you share from another app, links, posts, videos and screenshots, files it by topic on your phone, and brings it back in a short weekly list. Saving is free with no limit.

Saving something

In any app, tap Share and choose Tuck. If Tuck is missing from the share sheet, scroll to the end of the app row, tap More, and switch Tuck on.

Sync and moving to a new phone

Saves sync through your own iCloud account. On a new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID and open Tuck. If a save is missing, pull down on the home screen to sync again, and check that iCloud is switched on for Tuck in iOS Settings, your name, iCloud.

Export your saves

Settings, then Export everything. It makes one plain text file you can send anywhere. It is free.

Restore your purchase

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Cancel your subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Find Tuck and tap Cancel Subscription.

Contact

Email hello@usamamoin.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

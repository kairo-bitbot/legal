Privacy Policy for Tuck

Effective October 17, 2026. Tuck is published by Usama Moin ("we").

What the app stores

Tuck keeps what you save on your phone, in a local database: the link, text or screenshot you shared, its title and summary, the topic it was filed under, your note, the app it came from, and when it should come back. A screenshot you share is copied into the app's own storage. The share sheet hands each save to the app through a folder only Tuck and its share extension can read, and the extension makes no network requests.

Your saves also sync with your own private iCloud account (CloudKit), so they survive a new phone and reach your other devices. That copy sits on Apple's servers under your Apple ID. We cannot read it, and we run no server in between. Titles, summaries and the text read from screenshots are worked out on your phone, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else for that purpose.

Reminders and the weekly list are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. Tuck asks for notification permission the first time you set a reminder, and for nothing else.

What leaves your device

  • Your iCloud: the sync described above, and nothing else, goes to Apple.
  • The sites you save: to show a title, description and picture for a link, Tuck requests that page from its own site, the same request a browser makes. That site can see your phone's IP address. We never see the request or the page.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, never your name or payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Usage events: TelemetryDeck receives anonymous counts of what happens in the app: opening it, each onboarding step (or skipping it), viewing the subscription screen and which button opened it, starting a trial, buying or restoring a plan, and the rating prompt appearing. Each event carries a random identifier created on your device and hashed before it is sent. No link, title, note, topic or screenshot is ever included, and the events are not used for advertising or tracking. See telemetrydeck.com/privacy.
  • Crash reports: Sentry receives crash reports and session health (whether a session ended in a crash) to help us fix problems. A report includes the error and where in the code it happened, your device type and OS version, and never your saves or personal details. If a purchase hangs, the report also names the plan and carries the purchase library's recent log lines. We do not record your screen or your sessions, and we do not attach screenshots. See sentry.io/privacy.

Usage events and crash reports are on by default and controlled by one switch, Share anonymous usage data, in Settings. Turn it off and neither TelemetryDeck nor Sentry receives anything from then on. Apple, RevenueCat, TelemetryDeck and Sentry each handle their data under their own privacy terms. To ask us to delete anything tied to you, email hello@usamamoin.com.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. There is no account, no sign-in and no email address to give us.

Your data and deleting it

Export everything in Settings makes one plain text file of your saves and opens the iOS share sheet, so you choose where it goes. It is free.

Deleting the app removes the saves stored on your phone. It does not remove the copy in your iCloud. To delete that, open iOS Settings, tap your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Tuck, then Delete Data. Deleting a save inside Tuck removes it from your other devices too.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
